# Initiative brief

A foundation is considering a 12-month AI tutoring pilot for public libraries in two counties. The tool would help adults prepare for high-school equivalency exams. The foundation can fund software licenses, librarian training, and evaluation. It expects to work with eight libraries but has not signed agreements. No baseline completion rate, accessibility review, data-retention agreement, or participant compensation policy has been supplied. The team hopes to reach 600 learners and wants to decide whether to expand after six months.
