# AI tutoring public-benefit plan

## Decision brief

**Recommendation: Revise.** A limited pilot may proceed only after library commitments, accessibility review, data terms, baseline measurement, and learner participation in governance are defined.

**Outcome sentence:** Within 12 months, participating adult learners in two counties improve verified exam readiness and course persistence without disproportionate exclusion or privacy harm.

## Beneficiary and stakeholder map

| Group | Relationship | Voice or decision right |
|---|---|---|
| Adult learners | Primary beneficiaries; may bear privacy and access burdens | Paid advisory panel, complaints and appeal route |
| Learners with disabilities or limited English | At risk of exclusion | Accessibility and language testing before launch |
| Librarians | Operators and trusted intermediaries | Training feedback and pause authority |
| Foundation | Funder | Milestone release; cannot override safety stop |
| Libraries | Delivery partners | Local implementation approval; agreements **Unknown** |

## Theory of change

Funding enables licenses, training, support, and evaluation. These activities may produce accessible study sessions and timely guidance. **Assumption:** repeated supported use improves persistence and verified readiness. Test this against a baseline and segmented outcomes; reach alone does not establish benefit.

## Governance and participation

Create a paid learner advisory panel. Libraries approve local operation and may pause use. A named data steward controls access and deletion. Publish methods, segmented results, incidents, conflicts, and material scope changes. Provide human help plus complaints and appeals.

## Funding safeguards

Release funds after signed library agreements, accessibility and privacy reviews, and baseline approval. Restrict spending to documented licenses, training, learner participation, support, and evaluation. Require competitive or explained procurement, related-party disclosure, receipts, milestone evidence, and a written remaining-funds rule.

## Risk, pause, and stop register

| Risk | Affected group | Early signal | Trigger | Response | Accountable role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inaccessible interface | Disabled learners | Failed tasks or complaints | Critical barrier unresolved | Pause affected flow; provide human alternative | Accessibility owner |
| Sensitive-data misuse | All learners | Access or retention anomaly | Material incident | Stop collection, notify, investigate, delete as required | Data steward |
| Low learning benefit | Learners | No readiness improvement | Gate threshold missed twice | Stop expansion; redesign or exit | Evaluation lead |

## Pilot and exit plan

Start with two libraries after entry gates. Expand only after operational safety and early outcome evidence. If stopped, preserve human services, notify participants, export only agreed records, delete unnecessary personal data, publish findings, and redirect remaining funds under the approved rule.

## Open evidence requests

1. Signed partner commitments and delivery capacity.
2. Baseline readiness, persistence, access, and language needs.
3. Accessibility, privacy, retention, and vendor terms.
4. Named owners for evaluation, data, complaints, and stop authority.

## Recommendation

**Revise.** Reassess after the four evidence requests and pilot-entry safeguards are complete.
