# Planning rules

## Evidence labels

Use one label on every material claim:

- **Known** — supported by supplied evidence; cite the artifact or source.
- **Assumption** — plausible but unverified; state how to test it.
- **Commitment** — a proposed control or action with an owner and deadline.
- **Unknown** — missing information that may change the decision.

## Outcome quality

An outcome names a population, desired change, time horizon, and observable signal. Separate reach, use, service quality, behavioral change, and durable public benefit.

## Metric quality

Prefer 4–7 decision metrics. For each, record baseline, target, source, cadence, owner, segmentation, and gaming risk. Include at least one harm or exclusion metric and one beneficiary-reported measure.

## Governance minimum

Name who may approve, spend, change scope, access data, pause the pilot, and publish results. Define complaints and appeals, conflicts disclosure, beneficiary participation, incident response, independent review, and public reporting.

## Decision gates

- **Pilot entry:** minimum evidence and safeguards required before launch.
- **Continue:** evidence that justifies expansion.
- **Pause:** remediable threshold breach.
- **Stop:** unacceptable harm, infeasible delivery, invalidated theory of change, or persistent failure.
- **Exit:** data disposition, participant support, partner handoff, and remaining-funds treatment.
